# Report a press on the key window at its own place on macOS

On macOS, stock SDL reports a mouse button on the key window at SDL's stored cursor position, not at the event's own. That position is the last motion SDL saw, and it can be stale or another window's. For example, a window with a hit test drops motion over its draggable points, and also the first motion back onto a point that is not draggable. Move the pointer quickly from a draggable point onto a button, so that only that first motion arrives, and click. SDL's stored position is still the last point before the draggable one, and the press is reported there, missing the button. Or a transparent window of the app, shaped with `SDL_SetWindowShape`, lies over the key window. Cocoa still calls the shaped window's `mouseMoved:` for motion over the key window, so SDL's mouse focus and position stay on it. A press on the key window is then reported at the shaped window's position: as the shaped window's when that position lies outside the key window, and otherwise as the key window's, at the wrong place. A window with an XDL input region drops such motion instead, see `0015-window-input-region`.

This patch moves SDL to the button's own place first, as SDL's X11 backend does. Before a button of the key window at a point inside it, when SDL's mouse focus is another window or the stored position differs from the event's, it sends a motion to the window at the event's position. The focus moves to the window, and the button reports that window and its place. A button outside the window, such as a release after a drag, is reported as in stock SDL. Relative mode is left alone. A window that is not key already reports a button at the event's position, as in stock SDL.

## Testing

On macOS, run `testhittesting` with `SDL_EVENT_LOGGING=1`. Move the pointer quickly from a draggable area onto a normal point, logged as `HIT-TEST: NORMAL`, and click at once without moving. The `SDL_EVENT_MOUSE_BUTTON_DOWN` line gives the click's own place. Stock SDL gave the last point before the draggable area. It depends on timing, so it may take several tries.
